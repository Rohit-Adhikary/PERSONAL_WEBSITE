About Me

A bit about my journey

Background, philosophy, and what gets me excited to build.

I'm an undergraduate student studying Computer Science and Engineering at Gopalganj Science and Technology University. My journey into tech started with curiosity about how things work under the hood — from the logic gate and memory level all the way to complex algorithms and data-driven systems.

When I'm writing code, I value efficiency and clarity. Competitive programming has taught me to respect time limits, memory constraints, and corner cases. Every problem solved on Codeforces is a lesson in breaking down ambiguous puzzles into deterministic, step-by-step logic.

Outside of pure software, I love working with physical hardware. There's something deeply satisfying about writing C++ code that flashes onto an ESP32 chip, reads an ambient temperature sensor, and triggers a real-world switch over your local network.
